# v0.11.7 — Family Burst & Supplement UI Polish

Small family-alpha polish release on top of v0.11.6.

## Poke presentation
- Keeps the proven Web Animations API Poke rain engine from v0.11.6.
- Makes received Poke particles substantially larger (40–65 px) while keeping the popup above the rain.
- Reduce Motion still intentionally suppresses decorative rain.

## Large-family social bursts
- No backend event, Gold Day or Poke is discarded by this UI change.
- Fresh foreground social events are compacted before presentation when there is a large backlog.
- Pokes have presentation priority: up to three are shown individually; additional accepted Pokes are summarized into one Poke presentation.
- Three or more Gold Day events are summarized into one family Gold Day popup with names. Badge bursts are similarly summarized.
- Underlying inbox event IDs are marked seen only after the corresponding individual/summary presentation finishes.
- Existing backend protections are unchanged: the recipient Poke flood limit and mute controls remain authoritative.
- Owner-only temporary visual tests now include a local `15-person family burst` stress test (14 family Gold Days + 5 Pokes) that writes no Cloud data and sends nothing.

## Supplement UI
- `Review weekly supplements` now defaults closed instead of taking vertical space on page load.
- Once opened, it stays open through its own filter rerenders; the weekly supplement filter is wired to update the review.
- `Manage my supplements` receives a deliberate red management treatment and clearer gear label; destructive removal remains the stronger dedicated danger action.

## Deployment
Hosting-only. Firestore rules/indexes, Functions, Poke economics/flood limits, VAPID secrets and science scoring are unchanged.

# v0.16.0 — Activity detail, momentum, and family profile polish

v0.16.0 improves completed-activity recording and the Family profile without changing exercise IDs, mission scoring, Gold Day scoring, calorie estimates, or the underlying workout/hike collections.

## What changed

- Completed activities record precise start/end timestamps, calculated elapsed seconds, optional active/moving time, and exercise-specific optional tracker metrics.
- Running/walking use per-kilometre pace, rowing uses 500 m splits, and swimming uses 100 m pace.
- Heart-rate values are stored in a separate owner-only `privateWorkoutMetrics` document. They are absent from family workout payloads, diagnostics, missions, Gold Days, and calorie scoring.
- Family profiles combine safe shared workout and hike summaries into one date-sorted Recent activity section. Hike storage and badges remain separate.
- Nested exercise disclosures update their own DOM and ARIA state, preserving the outer Family disclosure.
- Weekly achievements were removed from the Family profile presentation; existing weekly summary data remains available to comparisons and calculations.
- Water and supplement entries read oldest to newest within the selected day.
- Discarding a workout resets the current Start Workout composer and keeps the user there, with Undo retained.
- Circuit and saved-routine builders reuse the categorized 156-exercise picker.
- Saved Routines Edit opens, scrolls to, and focuses the manager.
- The four-week Momentum display fills left to right and queues its 4/4 celebration through the existing presentation queue. Reduced-motion mode is static.
- Circuit Start and Skip controls meet the intended large touch-target layout on desktop as well as mobile.

## Privacy and security model

Public/shareable workout performance may include time, distance, pace, speed, elevation, cadence, pool/lap, stroke, power, and resistance data when the owner enters it. Heart rate is treated as private health data:

- reads and queries require `ownerId == request.auth.uid`;
- creates require the signed-in owner and active family identity;
- ownership, family, workout linkage, and schema cannot be changed;
- heart-rate values are bounded to 1–300 bpm;
- a family owner cannot read another member's private metrics.

The app still has zero browser runtime dependencies. Photos and exact GPX traces remain device-local.

## Research basis

The field model follows the distinction between elapsed and active/moving time used by established workout platforms and keeps exercise type, intensity, time, and objective performance separate. Advanced metrics are progressively disclosed and never treated as observed data unless the user enters them.

Primary references:

- [W3C Disclosure pattern](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/disclosure/)
- [W3C Disclosure card example](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/patterns/disclosure/examples/disclosure-card/)

# LogTogether v0.10.0 — Science Alpha

v0.10.0 changes the exercise-recording and motivational model while keeping the AppState storage schema at v1. Existing workout IDs, historical records, routines, Local/Cloud identity, authorization, offline storage, backups, family groups and visibility rules remain compatible.

## Science recording model

New exercise entries use `recordingProfileVersion: 2` and activity-appropriate profiles: resistance sets, skill-strength, isometrics, loaded carries, conditioning/sprint intervals, static/dynamic mobility, yoga flow, continuous cardio, swim sessions, lifesaving/water skills, combat rounds and skill drills.

Science-informed defaults are conservative UI starting points, not individualized prescriptions. Once a compatible exercise history exists, the composer prefers the user's recent actual setup. Changed-format legacy records are not silently reinterpreted.

## Progression

Progression is advisory and never automatic. Ordinary resistance exercises only show a progression prompt after two recent comparable sessions reach the top of the target rep range with all meaningful sets complete and effort <= 3/5. Repeated hard sessions favor maintaining the current setup. Mobility emphasizes control/consistency, cardio changes one variable at a time, and underwater skills never generate breath-hold/distance/PR progression prompts.

## Training analysis

Warm-up sets contribute zero training/mission credit. A meaningful resistance set contributes approximately 1.0 credit to its primary/direct category and approximately 0.5 to meaningful secondary/indirect categories. Normalized muscle charts remain display heuristics rather than literal physiological activation percentages.

## Gamification

- Gold Day: 1.0 Meaningful Activity, approximately 4 strength working sets, 20 cardio minutes, or 10 mobility minutes; mixed activity combines and the day caps at Gold.
- Weekly missions: Push, Pull, Lower, Core, Strength days, Cardio minutes, Mobility minutes, Gold Days, Personal activity, Hydration days.
- Difficulty changes motivational thresholds only; it does not relax safety rules.
- Self-rated effort is retained for history/progression but never multiplies mission points.
- Monthly badges use ~80% of all eligible weekly missions in the month, not a fixed 35; earned badges remain earned and store scoring version metadata.
- Four-week Momentum replaces a punitive daily streak.
- Calories remain approximate analytics, not achievement currency.
- Health-guideline progress is informational and separate from game completion.

## Safety

Underwater lifesaving/retrieval exercises never reward longer breath-holds, underwater distance, PRs or streaks. Aquatic skills display supervision/buddy reminders. Sparring does not gain points from greater contact intensity. High-skill/explosive movements prioritize quality before volume.

## Compatibility

- `schemaVersion` remains 1.
- New semantics are versioned through `scoringVersion: 2`, `exerciseDefaultsVersion: 2`, and per-exercise `recordingProfileVersion: 2`.
- Dates before the science cutover keep the legacy calendar interpretation.
- Legacy workouts/history remain rendered from the fields actually recorded.
- Legacy routines remain valid; newly saved routines preserve science profile metadata.
- Family weekly transport fields remain schema-v1 compatible; the UI avoids exposing legacy field names whose meaning changed in Science Alpha.

## Research basis

The in-app Settings → Calculations, game rules & version section explains the design and links to the primary references, including ACSM resistance-training guidance, fractional indirect-set research, WHO physical-activity guidance, the Adult Compendium, stretching/HIIT/sprint/jump-rope evidence, CDC aquatic safety guidance, gamification meta-analysis, self-determination theory, leaderboard heterogeneity, and streak/recovery research.

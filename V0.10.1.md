# LogTogether v0.10.1 — Science UX & Family Compare

v0.10.1 is a presentation and family-comparison refinement on top of the v0.10.0 Science Alpha. It does not change the AppState schema, exercise IDs, scoring version, exercise-defaults version, authorization model or core science calculations.

## Weekly Missions onboarding

The Home mission card now includes a four-page bottom-sheet tutorial. It explains the 10 weekly missions, Push/Pull/Lower/Core, Gold Days, 4-week Momentum, Health Guideline Progress and the fact that calories are trend estimates rather than game currency. The tutorial is intentionally concise; exact formulas, safety rules and research citations remain under Settings → Calculations, game rules & version.

## Cleaner Home science UI

Redundant calorie/game-rule copy under the daily activity card was removed. The long mission-credit explanation and long Health Guideline disclaimer were also removed from Home because those concepts now live in the tutorial and Settings. Immediate decision-making text, such as difficulty controls and safety information, remains where needed.

## History training mix

The existing Chest/Back/Shoulders/Arms/Legs/Core/Cardio/Mobility visualization remains because it answers a different question from Push/Pull/Lower/Core missions. It is now labelled **Training mix** with the subtitle **Estimated muscle training distribution** to make that distinction clear.

## Gold Day calendar semantics

Science-era Gold Days now use a fixed semantic gold fill and a star marker independent of the user's accent palette. Older pre-science goal-day history keeps its legacy interpretation. Exercise, weight and hike markers remain visible.

## Family Compare

Family comparison now opens on **Weekly activity**, not calories. It surfaces:

- Gold Days
- Weekly Mission completion
- Hydration target days
- Push / Pull / Lower / Core working credits
- Cardio and mobility minutes
- Strength-training days
- Estimated training distribution

Calories and water remain available in separate trend tabs. The calorie tab is labelled as an estimated trend and does not imply competition or mission scoring. Existing family privacy boundaries are retained: the UI uses only data already available through authorized family aggregates and shared activity records.

## Mobile polish

Family member-card chevrons were removed so long names, group labels and progress text have more room on narrow screens. The whole card remains tappable.

## Compatibility

- AppState schema remains v1.
- `scoringVersion` remains 2.
- `exerciseDefaultsVersion` remains 2.
- No Firestore rule/schema change is required.
- Existing v0.10 workouts, routines, badges and science cutover behavior remain compatible.

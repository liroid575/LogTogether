# LogTogether v0.10.2 — Weekly Missions Onboarding

v0.10.2 is a small onboarding refinement on top of the v0.10.1 Science UX build. It does not change scoring, exercise science, AppState schema, authorization, family aggregates, or Firestore rules.

## Changes

- Expanded the Weekly Missions tutorial from four to five concise swipe pages.
- Explicitly explains all ten weekly missions: Push, Pull, Lower body, Core, Strength days, Cardio minutes, Mobility minutes, Gold Days, Personal activity, and Hydration days.
- Clarifies that Strength days require at least four meaningful resistance working sets in a day.
- Clarifies how cardio, hiking, mobility, personal activity, and hydration count.
- Connects Gold Days directly to the Home progress card and includes a mixed-activity example.
- Renamed the Home `Meaningful activity today` section to `Today's Gold Day progress` so the 0–100% indicator has an obvious purpose.
- Retains the compact tutorial design and caps onboarding at five pages.
- Bumped the PWA shell cache to `logtogether-shell-v0.10.2-science-ux`.

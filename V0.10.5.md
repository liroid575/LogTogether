# LogTogether v0.10.5 — Family profile & workout polish

This patch prepares the Science Alpha for broader family testing. It keeps Science Scoring v2 and the ten-mission model unchanged while improving privacy controls, workout clarity, and family context.

## Family profile sharing
Each Cloud user can choose whether their family profile shows biological sex, this week’s supplement summary, recent workouts, and recent hikes. Existing users default to the previous visible behavior until they change a checkbox. Height, weight, photos, exact supplement timestamps and GPX traces remain private. Turning biological-sex sharing off removes that field from the family-readable member document; turning supplement sharing off publishes an empty weekly supplement summary. Workout/hike checkboxes control profile presentation while existing record-level visibility remains authoritative.

## Workout and Home polish
Personal activity is the final Weekly Mission. Saved routines use a horizontal swipe strip instead of growing vertically. Workout names have a pencil affordance. Science starter/history/progression guidance appears below Notes, while exercise-specific safety warnings stay beside the inputs where they matter.

## Family Compare
Weekly Activity now includes a secondary 4-week Momentum view. It shows how many of the last four weeks reached at least 8/10 missions, with no ranking or winner label.

## Security
Firestore member-presentation rules now allow one bounded `profileSharing` map containing exactly four booleans. No new collection is introduced and no height/weight/private-entry access is broadened.
